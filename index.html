MAKE IT YOURS

A closer look at anatomy.

Choose a study view, then select a structure on the model or in the list.

Move around
Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or use two fingers to pan.
See beneath the surface
Hover over a bone, nerve or gland for Hide this, Show only this and Study notes. Right-click opens the same menu. On touch screens, select a structure and use Hide or Show only in the study notes. Undo hide restores the last hidden part; Restore other structures exits solo view. Layer checkboxes remain available.
Focus on one structure
Select it and choose “Show only”. “Restore context” brings back your previous visibility choices.
Study dental nerves
The Dental nerves view reveals V2 and V3 pathways through transparent bones. These are sourced BodyParts3D 4.3 meshes registered to the skull. The model contains 26 structures. Independent lesser palatine and incisive meshes are not available in this source; the maxillary trunk includes its infraorbital continuation. Use the linked references for anatomical detail.
Keyboard
Tab through controls, Enter to select a structure, / to search, Esc to clear selection.

Labels name visible structures. Select the mandible, maxillae, palatine bones or sphenoid for dental surface landmarks. The Salivary glands view adds six glands and upper second molars. Open Duct openings for teaching diagrams of the cheek and floor of the mouth. This atlas is for anatomy study, not procedural planning.
