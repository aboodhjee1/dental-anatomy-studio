# Landmark evidence

Legacy bounding-box guesses are archived here and never rendered.
24 dental study pointers are visually localized on the source meshes in
anterior, lateral and superior projections: 10 mandible, 4 per maxilla,
4 sphenoid, and 1 horizontal plate per palatine bone.
Foramen pointers mark a surface rim; plates and mandibular angles identify
regions rather than exact clinical measurement points. References support
feature identity, not these specimen coordinates. Vertex indices, exact
mesh SHA-256, reference URLs and evidence descriptions are recorded in
 data/skull_content.json. Placements are not independently expert-validated.
Structure-name pointers identify whole named source meshes, not subfeatures.
